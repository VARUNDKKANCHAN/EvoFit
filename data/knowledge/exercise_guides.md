# EvoFit Expert Training Guide

This guide provides technical cues and safety standards for the five core barbell exercises tracked by the EvoFit system.

---

## 1. Back Squat (The King of Exercises)
**Primary Muscles**: Quadriceps, Glutes, Hamstrings, Erector Spinae.

### Technical Setup:
*   **Stance**: Feet slightly wider than shoulder-width, toes pointed out 15-30 degrees.
*   **Bar Placement**: High bar (traps) or Low bar (rear delts).
*   **Grip**: Narrow as possible without shoulder discomfort to create a "shelf."

### Coaching Cues:
*   **"Big Air"**: Take a deep breath into your belly and brace your core (Valsalva maneuver).
*   **"Sit Back"**: Initiate the movement by pushing your hips back slightly before bending knees.
*   **"Knees Out"**: Drive your knees in the direction of your toes to prevent collapse.
*   **"Chest Up"**: Maintain a proud chest throughout the descent.

---

## 2. Deadlift (The Foundation of Strength)
**Primary Muscles**: Posterior Chain (Glutes, Hamstrings, Lower Back), Forearms, Traps.

### Technical Setup:
*   **Stance**: Feet hip-width apart. The bar should be over the mid-foot.
*   **Grip**: Just outside the legs. Double overhand or mixed grip.
*   **Back**: Neutral spine. Avoid rounding the lower back.

### Coaching Cues:
*   **"Pack the Lats"**: Imagine squeezing oranges in your armpits to engage your upper back.
*   **"Push the Floor"**: Don't just "pull" the bar; push the world away with your legs.
*   **"Hips Through"**: As the bar passes your knees, snap your hips forward to lockout.
*   **"Drag the Bar"**: Keep the bar in contact with your shins and thighs at all times.

---

## 3. Bench Press (Upper Body Power)
**Primary Muscles**: Pectorals, Anterior Deltoids, Triceps.

### Technical Setup:
*   **Touch Points**: Eyes directly under the bar. Five points of contact (Feet, Glutes, Upper Back, Head).
*   **Arch**: A slight natural arch in the lower back is recommended for shoulder health.

### Coaching Cues:
*   **"Bend the Bar"**: Try to snap the bar in half to engage your lats and stable shoulders.
*   **"Leg Drive"**: Push your feet into the floor to create total body tension.
*   **"Touch and Go"**: Touch the bar to the lower sternum (nipple line) and explode up.
*   **"Elbows In"**: Tuck your elbows at a 45-degree angle; do not flare them at 90 degrees.

---

## 4. Overhead Press (Shoulder Stability)
**Primary Muscles**: Deltoids, Triceps, Traces, Core (Stabiilizers).

### Technical Setup:
*   **Stance**: Feet shoulder-width apart. Glutes and abs squeezed tight.
*   **Grip**: Slightly wider than shoulders. Forearms vertical.

### Coaching Cues:
*   **"Head Back"**: Move your face out of the way as the bar travels upward.
*   **"Punch the Ceiling"**: Drive the bar vertically in a straight line.
*   **"Look Through"**: Once the bar clears your head, push your head forward ("Look through the window").
*   **"Shrug at the Top"**: Shrug your shoulders slightly at lockout for maximal stability.

---

## 5. Barbell Row (Back Thickness)
**Primary Muscles**: Latissimus Dorsi, Rhomboids, Rear Deltoids, Biceps.

### Technical Setup:
*   **Stance**: Similar to deadlift. Torso should be as parallel to the floor as possible.
*   **Grip**: Pronated (palms down) or Supinated (palms up).

### Coaching Cues:
*   **"Elbows to Ceiling"**: Focus on driving your elbows back, not just pulling with your hands.
*   **"Pull to Hip/Belly Button"**: Pull the bar toward your lower ribs or navel for better lat engagement.
*   **"Squeeze Blades"**: Pin your shoulder blades together at the top of the rep.
*   **"Statue Stance"**: Minimize body momentum; do not use your legs to cheat the weight up.

---

## 6. Common Technical Faults (The "Don'ts")
*   **Squat**: "Good Morning" Squat (Hips rise way faster than chest). **Fix**: Drive back into the bar.
*   **Deadlift**: Lower back rounding (Cat back). **Fix**: Lower the weight and focus on "bracing."
*   **Bench**: Bouncing the bar off the chest. **Fix**: Control the descent (2-sec eccentric).
*   **OHP**: Excessive leaning back. **Fix**: Squeeze glutes like you're holding a coin between them.
*   **Rows**: Standing too upright (Turning it into a shrug). **Fix**: Stay parallel to the floor.

## 7. Nutrition for Strength & Hypertrophy
*   **Protein**: Aim for 1.6g to 2.2g of protein per kg of body weight. Essential for muscle repair.
*   **Carbohydrates**: Your primary fuel source. High-glycemic (white rice/fruit) around workouts.
*   **Fats**: Crucial for hormonal health. Focus on Omega-3s (Salmon/Walnuts).
*   **Hydration**: Drink 1L of water for every hour of intense training.

---

## 8. Recovery & Sleep
*   **Sleep**: Aim for 7-9 hours. Muscle is built while you sleep, not while you lift.
*   **Active Recovery**: On rest days, do light walking or mobility work.
*   **Deloading**: Every 4-8 weeks, reduce weight/volume by 50% to let joints recover.

---

## 9. Glossary of Terms
*   **RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion)**: A scale of 1-10 of how hard a set felt.
*   **PR (Personal Record)**: The most weight you've ever lifted for a specific rep count.
*   **Hypertrophy**: The enlargement of muscle tissue.
*   **Volume**: Sets x Reps x Weight. The primary driver of growth.
